## Grandpa's Cards: how many cards

Pick 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64 cards (phones stop at 32). A solved size shows a
green tick and its best time with that run's moves.

![Card count](17-cards-levels.jpg)

## Grandpa's Cards

Memory game by the sea in Alexandria: find each letter's capital and small
card. Your buddy runs to every card you tap, the other brother says the
letter's name; the timer, moves and pairs are on top.

![Cards](18-cards.jpg)
![Cards in English](19-cards-english.jpg)

## Grandpa's Cards: solved

Time, moves, the best time (or "New record!"), and sharing the result.

![Cards solved](20-cards-solved.jpg)

## Search the Monastery

Letters hide behind things in a monastery room, only part of each showing;
the ones to find are listed at the bottom with their names. Your buddy runs
to every letter you find and the other brother says its name; found letters
turn gold and are crossed out.

![Monastery](22-monastery.jpg)

## Snakes & Ladders (Level 1 challenge)

By the Pyramids, against the other brother (the computer) or a friend on
the same device. Before every roll a letter question pops up: a right
answer rolls the dice, a wrong one passes the turn. 64 squares with fixed
snakes and ladders; land exactly on 64 to win.

![Snakes & Ladders](23-snakes-ladders.jpg)

## Challenge picker (Level 1 challenge)

The Challenge stop now offers two games: Snakes & Ladders and Nono & Fofo
Adventure (more later). The stop gets its tick once both are done.

![Challenge picker](24-challenge-picker.jpg)
![Challenge picker in English](31-challenge-picker-english.jpg)

## Nono & Fofo Adventure: levels

Three levels (the Nile bank, the desert and the Pyramids, Alexandria at
night); each opens once the one before is finished. Best stars show under
each level, with up to 2 coral bonus stars.

![Adventure levels](25-adventure-levels.jpg)
![Adventure levels in English](32-adventure-levels-english.jpg)

## Nono & Fofo Adventure: playing

A side-scroller: small Fofo runs and jumps (arrows and space, or on-screen
buttons on touch screens), hits ? blocks for coins and the lost letters,
stomps enemies and grows into Nono with Grandpa's page. Letters found, lives
and coins are on top.

![Adventure on the Nile](26-adventure-nile.jpg)
![Adventure in the desert](29-adventure-desert.jpg)
![Adventure in Alexandria](30-adventure-alexandria.jpg)

## Nono & Fofo Adventure: finding a letter

A letter block asks a question first (a "?" pops out); only a right answer
shows the letter, which then goes into its slot.

![Letter question](27-adventure-question.jpg)
![Letter found](28-adventure-letter.jpg)

## Nono & Fofo Adventure: level complete

Stars from coins (plus 2 bonus stars for the hidden bonus letter), the
letters, coins and time, and a Share button for a picture of the result.

![Level complete](33-adventure-complete.jpg)

## Magic basket (Level 2: letter sounds)

The brother in the apple tree asks how the letter sounds and drops apples
with sounds on them; the child moves the basket (mouse, arrow keys or a
finger) to catch every sound of the letter. The top shows the apples caught
and a "?" chip per sound until it is caught, then the letter and its name.

![Magic basket](34-magic-basket.jpg)
